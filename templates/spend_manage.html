IT spend

Everything with a price — software, cloud, hosting, domains, plugins, and the invoices for things bought once. A recurring row is reduced to a run rate from its own billing cycle; a one-off sits in the month it was charged and adds nothing to the run rate. Either way the totals are derived rather than maintained by hand.
