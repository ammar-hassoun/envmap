{#- The live pod-log window, as one component. WHY IT IS AN INCLUDE AND NOT A PAGE Pod logs are reached from three places — the cluster page's pod list, the environment's pod table, and a direct link. Each had its own idea of what "logs" meant: one showed four hundred frozen lines with a button that navigated somewhere else to stream them, and streaming was something you had to ask for after arriving. Now every one of them opens this, already live. WHY IT RENDERS THE WAY IT DOES The old view rebuilt every line of its innerHTML on every arriving line. At a thousand buffered lines that is a thousand string concatenations and a full re-parse per line, and it is the whole reason the window felt slow. Nothing here re-renders: lines are appended to a fragment, the fragment is flushed once per animation frame no matter how many arrived, and the oldest nodes are dropped in bulk once the view is full. The result is that throughput costs frames, not squares: two thousand lines in a burst is still one flush. -#}
