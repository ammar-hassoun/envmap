# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build and run software together. Everybody who
takes part in its issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to keep it
a place where others want to take part too.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
- Assume the other person means well, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong and what would fix it.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a change, and say why you disagree
  once, plainly.
- Remember that people here have different levels of experience and different
  first languages.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks and harassment of any kind.
- Discrimination, or jokes at the expense of who somebody is.
- Publishing somebody's private information.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Repeatedly pushing after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere
somebody speaks for the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately. Use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on
this repository: that form is read only by the maintainer, and it is the
private channel for conduct reports as well until a contact address is
published on <https://envmap.tech>. Say what happened and where. Reports are
kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer reads every report and decides what is fair: a private word, a
warning, hiding or removing a comment, or blocking an account from the
repository.
