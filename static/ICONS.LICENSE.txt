Icons and vendor marks shipped in this product
==============================================

The artwork inlined into app/logodata.py, and served from /icons/logos.svg,
comes from three upstream sets. It is rebuilt by scripts/build_logos.py; the
packs themselves are not vendored, only the extracted result.

  Devicon        2.17.0   MIT           https://devicon.dev
                 the multi-colour vendor marks

  Simple Icons   16.28.0  CC0-1.0       https://simpleicons.org
                 single-path vendor marks, filled with each brand's own
                 registered hex from the pack's own metadata

  Lucide         1.30.0   ISC           https://lucide.dev
                 the line icons, which are not brands at all

ON THE MARKS THEMSELVES
Each vendor mark is the trademark of its owner and is used here only to
identify that owner's product in a list of integrations — nominative use. The
marks are not restyled, recoloured, stretched or combined, they never stand in
the brand slot a white-labelled install fills with the customer's own identity,
and none of it implies that those companies endorse or are affiliated with this
product. The one deliberate exception is a mono light cut of a mark that is
black or near-black, so it is visible on a dark background; every vendor
affected publishes such a cut for exactly that purpose. See INVERT in
scripts/build_logos.py.

Where a vendor publishes no mark either pack carries, nothing is drawn in its
place: that service gets a neutral lettermark instead.
